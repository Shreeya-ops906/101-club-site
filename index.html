Questions

Things worth asking.

How is this different from BNI?
BNI is built around weekly 7am meetings and category exclusivity — one accountant, one mortgage broker, one plumber per chapter. We're digital-first, nationally accessible, and built for ambitious modern founders rather than local service businesses. We're priced below BNI's all-in cost and our app is built for how founders actually work today.
How quickly will I see referrals?
Referrals compound over six to twelve months. The members who see them fastest are the ones who show up to monthly masterminds, complete five member one-to-ones in their first month, and post in the asks board. Most members close their first referred client within three months.
Is this London-only?
No. Digital-first from day one. Members across the UK — including Manchester, Bristol, Leeds, Edinburgh — participate in masterminds, accountability pods, and roundtables virtually. In-person meetups start in London and expand to other cities as member density grows.
What if my business isn't quite there yet?
The club is built for founders post-revenue, but we don't gate hard on turnover. What matters more is whether you have a real business, real clients to refer, and the time to engage. The application form gives us enough to make that call together.
Can I leave whenever I want?
Monthly plans cancel any time, no penalties. Annual plans give you a 17% discount and run for a year. If you don't win a client through the club in your first year on either plan, the one-client guarantee covers you for a full refund.
When will the premium tier launch?
Once we have 100 standard members in, we'll launch the Inner Circle tier — smaller vetted masterminds, quarterly dinners, 1:1 coaching, and direct founder access. Likely between months nine and twelve from launch. Standard members get first refusal.
