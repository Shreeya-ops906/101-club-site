## 2. Membership structure

Two tiers from day one, plus a time-limited founding-member offer.

- Founding-member badge in the app and directory
- Named on the founding-member wall at the first annual flagship event

### Standard — £129/month or £1,290/year

- Quarterly referral roundtables

### Premium / Inner Circle — £400/month or £4,000/year
*(Launches month 9–12 once 100+ Standard members are in)*

Everything in Standard, plus:
- Smaller, vetted masterminds (members must be £500k+ turnover)

- Profile elevation: podcast features, speaking slots, LinkedIn amplification
- 4× 1:1 coaching credits per year (with a vetted coach panel)
- Private WhatsApp with the founders

- *Target: established founders £500k+ turnover wanting deeper proximity and personal positioning*

## 3. What you actually get (the deliverable rhythm)

### Weekly
- **Accountability pods** — 30-min Zoom check-ins in groups of 4. Same people every week. Built around weekly commitments.
- **Wins feed** — members post deals closed, hires made, milestones hit in the app.

### Monthly
- **Mastermind session** — small group of 6–8, facilitated, hot-seat format. One member presents a challenge; the group works it.
- **Masterclass** — 45-min guest expert session on a specific topic (sales, hiring, pricing, marketing, fundraising, ops).
- **Member spotlight** — featured member interview, surfaced in the app and newsletter.

### Quarterly
- **Referral roundtable** — structured speed-networking by category. Designed to spark ICP matches and warm intros.
- **Themed half-day virtual event** — deeper dive on one growth topic.
- **Member meetup (in-person)** — once 50+ members in a city. London first. Pay-your-own-bar drinks.

### Annually
- **Flagship event** — 150–300 attendees, full day, headline speakers, deal dinners. Year-end. The community's "Super Bowl."

## 4. The app — your moat

You don't build a custom app on day one. You launch on Circle.so or Skool, then build the custom referral app as the "Version 2" relaunch moment around month 9–12.

### MVP feature set (Phase 1, off-the-shelf stack):
- Member directory with ICP profile fields
- Discussion spaces by sector (SaaS, agencies, e-commerce, services, trades, etc.)
- Event RSVPs and recordings
- Resource library (masterclass replays, frameworks, templates)
- Stripe-powered membership billing

### Custom app features (Phase 2):
- **Smart referral matching** — members tag ideal client profile; app pings matches when relevant
- **One-tap warm intros** — pre-templated, member-to-member intro flows
- **Referral value tracker** — log a referral, log when it closes, track £ generated
- **"Asks" board** — post a specific need, app pushes to relevant members
- **One-to-one booking** — calendar-integrated, prompts new members to do 5 in month one
- **Reciprocity insights** — soft accountability showing who refers to whom
- **Live wins feed** — celebrate deals, drive aspiration and FOMO
- **Event check-in & contact swap** — QR code-based, walk away with everyone's contact details

## 10. The risks and how you handle them

**Risk 1: Referrals take time to materialise.**
*Mitigation:* Set expectations in onboarding — "Referrals compound over 6–12 months." Make masterminds, events, and access feel valuable from day one so members don't churn while waiting.

**Risk 2: Cold-start problem — app needs density per category.**
*Mitigation:* Focus founding 100 on 5 core categories first (e.g. agencies, SaaS, professional services, e-commerce, trades). Don't try to be everywhere.

**Risk 3: Digital-only feels thin past 100 members.**
*Mitigation:* The 50-member event unlock builds in the in-person muscle early. By month 6 you have monthly in-person meetups in London.

**Risk 4: Churn at month 3–6 when monthly billing renews.**
*Mitigation:* Push annual hard at signup. Set up automated "30-day check-in" and "90-day review" calls to catch unhappy members before they cancel.

**Risk 5: Founder burnout — you're the face, the salesperson, the facilitator, the operator.**
*Mitigation:* Hire community manager at month 9 (not later). Use PT mastermind facilitators from month 4. Protect 1 day a week for content creation and 1 day a week for thinking.
